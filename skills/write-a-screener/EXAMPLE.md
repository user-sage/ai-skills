# Example of usage: Write a screener

This example is illustrative. Anything in square brackets is for the person to supply. It contains no real participants, quotes or numbers.

## The request

> Write a screener for people who bought running shoes online in the last 6 months.

## The output

# Screener: Buying running shoes online

## Who we are looking for
People who bought running shoes online for themselves in the last 6 months. [Add any segment from the Brief, such as new runners and regular runners.]

## Questions

1. Which of these have you bought online in the last 6 months? Select all that apply.
   Type: multiple choice, random order
   Options:
   - Running shoes (qualifies)
   - Hiking boots
   - Football boots
   - Casual trainers
   - Sports clothing
   - None of these (ends the screener)

2. When did you last buy running shoes online?
   Type: single choice
   Options:
   - In the last month (qualifies)
   - 1 to 3 months ago (qualifies)
   - 4 to 6 months ago (qualifies)
   - 7 to 12 months ago (ends the screener)
   - More than a year ago, or never (ends the screener)

3. Who were they for?
   Type: single choice, random order
   Options:
   - Myself (qualifies)
   - Someone else in my household (ends the screener)
   - A gift (ends the screener)
   - I don't remember (ends the screener)

4. Where did you buy them? Select all that apply.
   Type: multiple choice, random order
   Options:
   - A sports brand's own website (qualifies)
   - A general online retailer (qualifies)
   - A specialist running shop's website (qualifies)
   - [A shop name that does not exist] (decoy, ends the screener)
   - Other (qualifies, with a text box)

5. How often do you run?
   Type: single choice
   Options:
   - Several times a week (qualifies, regular runner quota)
   - About once a week (qualifies, regular runner quota)
   - A few times a month (qualifies, new or occasional runner quota)
   - Rarely or never (qualifies, new or occasional runner quota)

6. Do you or anyone in your household work in any of these? Select all that apply.
   Type: multiple choice
   Options:
   - Market research or user research (ends the screener)
   - A sports or footwear brand or retailer (ends the screener)
   - Marketing or advertising (ends the screener)
   - None of these (qualifies)

7. In a sentence or two, tell us how you chose the last pair of running shoes you bought online.
   Type: open text
   Review by hand. Keep people who describe a real purchase. Remove answers that are vague or copied.

## Answer key
| Question | Qualifying answers | Ends the screener | Why it matters for the Brief |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Running shoes | None of these | Confirms the core behaviour without naming it in the question |
| 2 | Last month to 6 months ago | 7 months ago or longer | Recency, so the purchase is fresh in memory |
| 3 | Myself | Someone else, a gift, don't remember | The person made the choice themselves |
| 4 | Any real shop, or Other | The made-up shop | Decoy, catches people who tick everything |
| 5 | Any | None | Sets the quota group |
| 6 | None of these | Any industry option | Removes people with professional knowledge |
| 7 | A specific, real description | Vague or copied text | Catches people who qualify on paper only |

## Decoy
Question 4 includes a shop that does not exist. Anyone who picks it is guessing or ticking every box, so they end the screener. [Replace the placeholder with a made-up name that sounds real in your market.]

## Disqualifiers
- Working in research, a sports or footwear brand or retailer, or marketing, because their answers reflect professional knowledge.
- [Taking part in a study about running or footwear in the last [3] months, if your panel tracks this.]
